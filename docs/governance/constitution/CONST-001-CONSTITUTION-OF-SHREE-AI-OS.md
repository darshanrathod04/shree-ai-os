# Preamble

We, the builders of Shree AI OS, establish this Constitution to define the identity, purpose, principles, governance, and long-term direction of the platform.

Shree AI OS exists to build an AI Operating Platform founded on engineering excellence, durable architecture, shared knowledge, and disciplined execution.

We believe that intelligent software platforms are not created solely through code. They are created through thoughtful architecture, stable contracts, documented knowledge, and continuous learning.

This Constitution serves as the highest governing document of Shree AI OS. Every architectural decision, engineering standard, implementation practice, and future evolution of the platform shall remain consistent with the principles established herein.

---

# Article I — Platform Identity

## Section 1 — Official Name

The official name of the platform shall be:

**Shree AI OS**

---

## Section 2 — Nature of the Platform

Shree AI OS is an AI Operating Platform.

It is not an AI chatbot.

It is not an AI agent.

It is not a single application.

It is a platform designed to provide reusable kernels, stable contracts, engineering standards, and intelligent capabilities for building future AI-powered software systems.

---

## Section 3 — Scope

The platform shall provide architectural foundations for:

- Cognitive Intelligence
- Runtime Execution
- Memory Management
- Planning
- Autonomy
- Capabilities
- SDKs
- Developer Tooling

The platform shall remain modular and extensible.

---

## Constitutional Principle

> **Shree AI OS is built as a platform first and an application second.**

---

## Rationale

Applications solve today's problems.

Platforms enable tomorrow's solutions.

Every engineering decision shall strengthen the platform before expanding individual features.

---

# Article II — Platform Vision

## Vision Statement

## Constitutional Principle
